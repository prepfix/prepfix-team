How to format the songs
Put each song one after another. Separate songs with a line of === The first line is the title. Video links are optional - use one Link: line for each link. Amazing Grace Link: https://youtu.be/XXXXXXXXXXX Link: https://youtu.be/YYYYYYYYYYY Amazing grace, how sweet the sound That saved a wretch like me I once was lost, but now am found Was blind, but now I see === Another Song Title First line of lyrics here Second line here

Pasting from WhatsApp? Just paste it as it is. The date, time and sender name are removed automatically, the link message is attached to the song that follows it, the first line of the lyrics message becomes the title (a trailing word "Lyrics" is dropped), and the rest is the lyrics.

A blank line inside a song starts a new stanza. Extra blank lines are removed. You can also upload a .txt file in this format, a .csv file with columns title, lyrics, link (several links can be separated by spaces or semicolons), or a .json list of songs.

Replacing keeps the old song's number. It gets the new title, lyrics and video, and its old video links stay as extra links. (Songs with the same title but different lyrics are always added as new songs.)

Add video links

Paste YouTube links, one per line. You can paste straight from WhatsApp. The app reads each video's title and finds the song it belongs to. Check the matches below, fix any that are wrong by typing the right song number, then save.

To choose the song yourself, put its number or title on the same line as the link, e.g. 12 https://youtu.be/... or Amazing Grace https://youtu.be/...
